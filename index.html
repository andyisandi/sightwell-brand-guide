Logo & Usage

The SIGHTWELL mark is the primary identifier. Use it with consistent clear space and only on approved background colors. Never distort, recolor, or place it on competing patterns.

Clear space

Hold a margin of clear space around the lockup equal to the cap-height of SIGHTWELL on every side. Nothing — type, image or edge — enters that zone.

Minimum size

Never render the full lockup below 120px wide on screen. Below that, the wordmark may stand alone with the gold rule removed.

Do

  • Use the primary cream + amber lockup on ink wherever possible.
  • Reverse to ink + amber on cream when a light ground is required.
  • Use the all-ink or all-cream lockup on brand color grounds.
  • Keep the wordmark, gold rule and STUDIOS line locked together.

Don't

  • Recolor the lockup outside the approved palette.
  • Stretch, rotate, condense or re-space the mark.
  • Separate STUDIOS or the gold rule from the wordmark.
  • Place it on busy imagery or low-contrast grounds.
04

Brand Strategy

Sightwell Studios helps people and organizations see what they've been missing — and act on it. The brand stands for clear-eyed thinking, defiance of the default, and progress that is earned through visibility.

The core conviction

We illuminate what others miss.

The balance of light

We use the metaphor of light deliberately — not the dramatic flash of revelation, but the steady, calibrated illumination that reveals what was always there. Our work is precise and purposeful.

Defying the default

Progress requires first seeing and then defying the default setting that's keeping you stuck. The systems around us are built to keep you moving toward someone else's plan. We believe in another way.

05

Voice & Tone

Voice is consistent — it's who we are. Tone adapts to context. We are direct without being blunt, warm without being soft, and confident without being loud.

We say the one thing plainly enough to act on.

Clear, not clever

We don't hide meaning behind language. Every sentence earns its place. If it doesn't move the reader forward, it doesn't belong.

Confident, not loud

We trust the work to speak. Our voice doesn't need to shout — it simply states what it knows, precisely and without apology.

Purposeful empathy

We understand what keeps people stuck. We speak to that honestly, without condescension. We illuminate, we don't lecture.

Permission to act

The goal of every communication is momentum. We want the reader to feel capable and motivated — not just informed.

Grids

Layouts sit on a consistent grid so spacing and alignment stay disciplined across every format. Five structures cover most needs — manuscript, columns, modular, split, and hybrid. Pick one grid per layout and hold to it.

07

Icons

Icons use a consistent 1.5px stroke weight at 32×32px. They are geometric, minimal, and purposeful — never decorative. Use sparingly and only when they clarify, not when they decorate.

Applications

The brand system applies consistently across digital and print touchpoints. These examples demonstrate the palette, typography, and logo working together in context.

Website

Homepage — desktop

The website leads with the brand's core promise — "We illuminate what others miss" — set in the serif voice on a charcoal hero. Navigation stays minimal, the amber call-to-action carries the single next step, and generous space keeps the page calm. Shown here as direction, still in progress.

LinkedIn company page & post cards
